Divergence 7.1: Typographic Fallbacks

CSS Fonts 4 §5 (0.5 × em) vs. Blink SimpleFontData (0.5 × ascent)

When the 'x' glyph is missing from a font, CSS Fonts 4 mandates falling back to 0.5 × font-size (0.5em). Chromium Blink falls back to 0.5 × ascent. For standard fonts where ascent is 80% of em, this causes a 20% height deficit.

W3C CSS Fonts 4 §5 Fallback (100px)

Spec: 10ex 100px (5em)
Formula: 10 × (0.5 × 20px) = 100px
✓ Normative Dimension: Strictly scales with the declared font-size (em metric).

Chromium Blink (simple_font_data.cc: 80px)

-20px Deficit
Blink: 10ex 80px (4em)
Formula: 10 × (0.5 × 16px) = 80px
✗ 20% Deficit Error: In simple_font_data.cc, Blink uses 0.5 * ascent. Modeled in BlinkCSSUnitResolutionSemantics.v.
