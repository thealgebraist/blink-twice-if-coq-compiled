1.1 Perianth: Calyx and Corolla

Together, the calyx (sepals) and corolla (petals) constitute the perianth, the outer non-reproductive envelope of the flower. The calyx protects the developing floral tissues in the bud stage, while the pigmented corolla exhibits spectral absorption and UV reflection patterns designed to attract specialized animal pollinators.

1.2 Reproductive Organs: Androecium & Gynoecium

The fertile organs comprise the male androecium (stamens bearing pollen sacs) and the female gynoecium (one or more carpels enclosing ovules). Double fertilization within the ovary produces the diploid zygote and triploid nutritive endosperm.
